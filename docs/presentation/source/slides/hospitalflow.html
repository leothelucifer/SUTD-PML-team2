From the bedside to the model

1 · At the bedside

Monitor: HR, SpO2, BP, resp rate

Nurse: temperature, observations

Blood draw: every few hours

2 · Hospital systems

EHR flowsheet: nurse validates vitals

Lab system: 26 lab results

Registration: age, sex, admit time

3 · Integration

Integration engine collects HL7 v2 / FHIR messages and calls the model each hour over VPN

4 · Hourly window

One row per ICU hour, same binning and units as training; the last 7 rows go with each call

5 · Model and alert

Risk score each hour; alert in the EHR or nurse dashboard; clinician decides

Our dataset is step 4: PhysioNet extracted these hourly rows from two hospitals' records.

Labels arrive late: Sepsis-3 onset is confirmed only after cultures and antibiotics are recorded.
