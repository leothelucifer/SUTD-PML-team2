Objective and metrics

Unit of prediction

One ICU hour. At hour t, the probability that this hour carries the challenge's sepsis label, using data up to t only. The label turns on 6 h before onset.

Primary metric

Challenge clinical utility score: rewards early alerts, penalises late, missed and false ones. AUROC, AUPRC and Brier score also reported.

Operational limit

At most 0.2 false alerts per non-septic patient-day, with a 6-hour cooldown. The threshold is the highest-utility cut-off inside that limit.

Scope: research and engineering on de-identified public data. Decision support only; not a medical device. The 0.2 budget is a placeholder until clinicians set one.

3

The score is not "probability of onset in the next 6 hours": the label stays on after onset, so say "the challenge label". The alert budget, not AUROC, decides whether this would be usable. Without a cooldown, a patient above threshold for five hours would count as five alarms; clinically it is one.
