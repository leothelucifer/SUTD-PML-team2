The problem

4–8%

higher risk of death for each hour that sepsis treatment is delayed

Sepsis is hard to spot early: vital signs drift slowly and labs arrive every few hours, not every hour.

Early-warning scores exist, but a tool that fires too often gets ignored. Alarm fatigue is the real constraint.

This project: an hourly risk score that warns early enough to act, without flooding the ward.

Sources: Kumar et al., Crit Care Med 2006 (≈7.6% per hour of antibiotic delay); Seymour et al., NEJM 2017 (≈4% per hour) · 2
