The bar to beat: the SIRS rule

Alert when ≥ 2 of 4 hold

  • Temp > 38 or < 36 °C
  • HR > 90 beats/min
  • Resp > 20 or PaCO2 < 32
  • WBC > 12 or < 4 ×10⁹/L

Uses the last value seen; never measured counts as normal. Band neutrophils are not recorded.

At the clinical rule, SIRS flags about half of septic-labelled hours but raises 1.98 false alerts per non-septic patient-day, ten times our 0.2 budget.

Validation split only; the rule is fixed at ≥ 2, not tuned · 10

SIRS is never trained, so it uses no data to fit. It goes through the same threshold, cooldown and metric code as the models. The sweep over 1 to 4 criteria is descriptive: the rule stays at 2.
